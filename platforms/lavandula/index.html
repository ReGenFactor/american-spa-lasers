02

Why 1927 nm

Platform literature describes 1927 nm as suited to the appearance of pigmentation with a lower risk of post-inflammatory change than more aggressive CO2 or Er:glass platforms. That is a literature statement, not an American Spa Lasers study.

03

Current generation versus earlier systems

Earlier ablative platforms such as fractional CO2 or Er:YAG deliver deeper resurfacing with longer downtime and higher PIH risk on a wider range of skin types. Lavandula uses 1927 nm thulium in a non-ablative fractional approach targeting water. Owners offer shorter recovery (literature states 1–3 days of mild redness), broader suitability, and a machine that supports higher daily throughput for brightness, tone, and fine lines.
