Questions owners ask

Is this Quadlaze Max?

No. Quadlaze Max is the 1,200 W and 2,000 W pair on the main line. HIDL 1800 is this 1,800 W cart with four changeable spots.

How is it different from AlphaLux?

AlphaLux is the ALX-68 family, with 6 mm, 12 × 12 mm, and 12 × 20 mm windows, in 1,000 W and 2,000 W heads. HIDL 1800 is one 1,800 W handle with four different spots, including 13.5 × 38.5 mm.

Who can operate it?

Trained clinicians only. Class 4 laser. Wavelength-rated eyewear and the instructions for use are required. A state laser licence applies where the practice is.

Are the photographs results?

No. They show the machine. They are not a before-and-after and not a clinical study.
