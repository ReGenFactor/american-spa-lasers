Questions owners ask

How is UniLux different from AlphaLux?

Both are triple-wavelength diode carts. UniLux is offered in 1,200 W, 1,600 W, and 2,000 W builds. AlphaLux is the ALX-68 family in Trio, Pro, and Core.

Who can operate it?

Trained clinicians only. It is a Class 4 laser. Operators wear the wavelength-rated eyewear supplied with the system and follow the instructions for use.

Are the photographs treatment results?

No. They are product photographs. They are not a before-and-after and not a clinical study.

What is the call?

A technology discussion on wavelengths, spot sizes, and which power build fits the practice. It is not a clinic demo.

Contraindications

Not for cancer, particularly skin cancer; injury in the treatment area; prior laser or electrolysis hair removal in that area; pregnancy, including IVF, lactation, or plans to conceive; severe medical conditions including hormonal disorders stimulated by bright light; bleeding, cardiac, or renal disorders; known photosensitivity or photosensitive medicines and herbs relevant to these wavelengths; active infection; dermatitis, pigmented disorders, or tumors in the area; history of keloid or hypertrophic scarring; sun exposure or tanning in the past 30 days; artificial tanning in the 3 to 4 weeks before or after treatment; anticoagulants; steroids within 30 days; any systemic retinoid in the previous six months; immune deficiency or immunosuppressive agents; endocrine disorders stimulated by these wavelengths; uncontrolled diabetes; fragile or very dry skin; epilepsy; coagulopathies; tattoos or permanent makeup in the treatment area; anyone under 18. Read the instructions for use supplied with UniLux for the full list.
