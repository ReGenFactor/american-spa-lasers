Two wavelengths, then options

1,064 nm and 532 nm are the working pair. 585 nm and 650 nm are optional heads for colours the main pair is not aimed at. The cellular handle is the rejuvenation option on the same console.

What an owner can offer

A pigment and tattoo-ink appointment, separate from the 21-day skin course. Talk about appearance. Do not promise clearance, and do not call it a cure.

Faster than a nanosecond sheet

Current generation versus earlier systems

Earlier Q-switched nanosecond lasers required more sessions and carried higher risk of thermal damage on pigment work. Questions owners ask

Is this RenuVa?

No. RenuVa is the non-ablative fractional erbium. Pico Nd:YAG is a picosecond platform for pigment and tattoo-ink appearance.

Can every colour be treated?

The sheet assigns 532 nm to red, brown, and orange ink, 1,064 nm to black and blue, 585 nm to red and purple, and 650 nm to green. Those are the printed aims. They are not a guarantee.

Who may buy it?

Practices in Australia and the United States that hold the laser authority the state requires. Trained operators. Wavelength-rated eyewear.

Are there before-and-after photos?

No. This page shows the machine only.
