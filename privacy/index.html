Personal information is information or an opinion about an individual whose identity is apparent, or can reasonably be worked out, from that information. Regen Factor Pty Ltd collects personal information only by lawful and fair means, and only what we need to answer an inquiry or to supply what you order. Where it is reasonable and practical, we collect it from you.

The request form does not store the form on our server. Sending it opens your own email program with a message addressed to info@pepfactor.org. We receive those details only if you send that email.

We do not appoint a third party to collect that form. If you press play on a film, the player loads from YouTube and YouTube receives that request under its own policy.

We use personal information to:

  • follow up the inquiry you started

We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for cross-context advertising.

Regen Factor Pty Ltd may disclose personal information:

You may ask for access to the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by emailing info@pepfactor.org. We do not charge a fee for the request or for a correction. We will answer within a reasonable time.

If we no longer need the information for a purpose in this policy, and the law does not require us to keep it, we will take reasonable steps to destroy it or to de-identify it. We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information we hold from misuse, interference, loss, and from unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

In the United States you may use the same email to ask for access, correction, or deletion. We will honour a request the law of your state requires us to honour.

You may opt out of sales follow-up at any time by emailing info@pepfactor.org. Until you do, we may reply and follow up the inquiry you sent. Opting out does not delete a record the law says we must keep, and it does not cancel an order. Cancellation is dealt with in the cancellation policy.
