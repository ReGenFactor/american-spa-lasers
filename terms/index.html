2. The contract

The contract replaces earlier discussions about the same order, including talk about how a system will perform or what result a client of the clinic should expect. It overrides the buyer’s purchasing terms, whether or not those terms say they prevail. Unless we agree in writing, these are the only terms on which we supply goods or services.

A page on this site is not the order. Published prices on the site are United States offers. Australian prices are quoted. Specifications can change by configuration.

4. Cancellation

Once an order is placed and the deposit is paid, the buyer cannot cancel. The deposit is not refunded. The balance remains payable. An order is placed when we accept it in writing and the deposit on the quotation or invoice has been paid. A change of mind is not a ground for cancellation.

If we cannot supply the order, we will say so and refund the deposit. If we agree in writing to a cancellation we are not obliged to accept, the buyer pays our loss on that order, including goods already ordered from a supplier and costs already incurred, and we may keep the deposit toward that loss.

Nothing in this clause removes a right the Australian Consumer Law does not allow us to remove, or a right of the United States that cannot be waived, including a right to cancel under the FTC Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule where that rule applies and we do not ship in the time it requires. The price is our quoted price, which binds us for 30 days from the date of the quote and may be accepted in that time, or if there is no current quote, our price at delivery. Unless the quote says a tax is included, sales tax, GST, and similar taxes are extra. Payment is by the method we direct. A cheque or other instrument is not received until it clears.

If a payment is late, we may charge interest from the due date until payment, calculated daily. On an Australian order the rate is 2% per year above the Commonwealth Bank of Australia business overdraft reference rate. On a United States order the rate is the lesser of 1.5% per month and the highest rate the law of the state of delivery allows. Interest is payable on demand.

If a supply under the contract is a taxable supply under A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999 (Cth), the buyer pays GST in addition to the price, unless the price is stated to include GST, at the same time as the price. On a delivery in the United States, the buyer pays the sales tax that applies to that sale. We do not charge Australian GST on a supply that is not a taxable supply.

We ship to clinic addresses in Australia and the United States. We do not ship these systems to other countries. We will use commercially reasonable efforts to deliver by the date on the order. The buyer gives access to the premises. Delivery to a person the buyer names is delivery to the buyer. Where a freight rate is weight-based, United States weights are rounded up to the next full pound, and Australian weights are rounded up to the next kilogram the carrier bills.

Except where a consumer guarantee or the FTC shipping rule says otherwise, a late delivery is not a repudiation of the contract, and we are not liable for consequential loss from late delivery. The refund pages state the shipping practice for Australia and the United States.
